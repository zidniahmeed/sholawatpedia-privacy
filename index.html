Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

SholawatPedia is a free community‑driven encyclopedia of Sholawat (Islamic devotional songs). This page explains how we collect, use, store and protect the personal and device data you provide while using the mobile app.

What data do we collect?

We only collect data that is necessary to provide the core features of the application. The categories are:

  • Account information – When you create a contributor or user account we store your name, email address and a password hash. This data is required for authentication, linking bookmarked sholawat and showing your contributions.
  • Bookmarks / offline library – When you download a sholawat for offline reading we keep a reference to the downloaded file on your device. No personal data is sent to our servers for the actual file; only a local identifier is stored.
  • Donation button interaction – The "Donate" (heart‑handshake) button does not transmit personal data. It simply opens a web‑view to the external donation page.
  • Notifications – If you enable push notifications we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging token linked to your account. The token lets us send you moderation or contribution status updates.
  • Analytics & crash reporting – Using Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics we collect anonymous usage events (screen views, button taps) and crash logs. No email, name or location data is included in these logs.
  • Device information (read‑only) – The app reads the device’s OS version, language preference and a generated instance ID. This data is used only for debugging and to ensure compatibility.

How do we use the data?

  • To authenticate you and keep your session active.
  • To display the list of sholawat you have bookmarked or downloaded for offline use.
  • To let you receive push notifications about moderation results or new content.
  • To improve the app through aggregated, anonymized analytics.
  • To diagnose crashes and stability issues.

Data sharing

We do **not** sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for advertising. The only external services that receive data are:

  • Google Play Services / Firebase – for authentication, push messaging and analytics.

All data transferred to these services is limited to the items described above.

Data retention and deletion

  • Account data (email, name, password hash) is retained until you delete your account from the app’s Settings screen.
  • Bookmarks and offline files are stored locally on your device; you can clear them from the "Offline Library" screen.
  • Analytics data is retained according to Firebase’s default retention policy (up to 14 months).
  • Push‑notification tokens are cleared automatically when you log out.

Security

All communication between the app and our backend uses HTTPS with TLS 1.2+. Passwords are stored as salted hashes; we never keep plain‑text passwords. Local files are saved in the app’s private storage area, inaccessible to other apps without root access.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for users of all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years old. If a child’s data is discovered, it will be deleted promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Please review it periodically.
